Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed by us and highlights the importance we place on the privacy of our users. It explains how we handle privacy, data usage, and third-party services. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how information is processed.

1. Data Collection

We do not intentionally collect personal data directly from users through our applications. Under no circumstances do we access, store, or share your personal or device information. Your data remains entirely under your control.

However, our applications may use third-party services such as Google AdMob, which may process limited data such as:

  • IP address
  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • Usage and interaction data for advertising and measurement purposes

These data are processed by third-party providers in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

2. Use of Data

Any limited data processed by third-party services may be used for:

  • Ad delivery and frequency control
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Aggregated analytics and performance measurement
  • Improving ad relevance where user consent is provided

We do not use collected data for profiling beyond third-party advertising systems.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may include third-party services such as Google AdMob (advertising and analytics). These services may collect and process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review their policies for more information.

No data we control is shared with third parties. Any data processing by third-party services is governed by their own policies and applicable regulations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies directly in our mobile applications. No tracking information is stored on your device or our servers by us during your usage.

However, third-party services may use standard identifiers and tracking technologies for functionality and advertising purposes in accordance with their own policies.

5. User Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we comply with applicable data protection regulations such as GDPR. Where required, we obtain and respect user consent before processing personal data for advertising and measurement purposes. Users may modify or withdraw consent at any time through device settings or consent management tools provided within the application.

6. Data Storage

We do not store personal data on our servers. Some application-related data may be stored locally on the user's device to enable functionality. This data remains under the user's control, remains unseen and unshared by us, and can be removed at any time by:

  • Clearing app data via device settings
  • Uninstalling the application

7. Permissions

Some of our applications may request specific permissions (such as internet access) solely to enable core functionality. These permissions are strictly used to ensure the functionality of the app and are not connected to your personal data beyond the scope required for the application to function.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal user data. If any data processed by third-party services is subject to retention policies, it is handled according to those providers' policies.

Users may request information or deletion requests by contacting us at: meftuntech@gmail.com. Requested data deletion will be completed within 30 days where applicable. Users may also clear local app data at any time through their device settings or by uninstalling the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date on the application or distribution page. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at: meftuntech@gmail.com.

Summary

We prioritize user privacy, minimize data usage, and rely on trusted third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising functionality under user consent where required. All features in our applications are developed with a privacy-first approach.
